Delete your ShutterCat account

You can permanently delete your account from the ShutterCat web app or Android app.

  1. Sign in to the ShutterCat account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → My account → Delete account and all data.
  3. Enter your current password, accept the deletion warning, and confirm.
Open ShutterCat

Deletion removes the account, sessions, email verification records, tasks, cats, coins, subscription entitlement records, and locally stored proof photos on that device. Security rate-limit records that cannot identify the account expire automatically.

Google Play subscription: deleting your ShutterCat account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel it in Google Play before deleting the account.

If you cannot sign in, use the password reset flow first. A public support address will be added before production launch.

删除 ShutterCat 账号

登录需要删除的账号，打开“设置 → 我的账号 → 删除账号和全部数据”，输入当前密码并确认。账号、会话、验证记录、任务、猫猫、喵币、订阅权益记录以及该设备本地保存的凭证照片将被永久删除。

注意：删除 ShutterCat 账号不会取消 Google Play 订阅，请先在 Google Play 中管理或取消订阅。
